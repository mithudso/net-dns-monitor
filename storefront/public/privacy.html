Privacy

Website and purchases

Cloudflare serves this site and processes requests, including information such as your IP address. If you buy Premium, Stripe or PayPal processes your payment under its own privacy policy. Net DNS Monitor does not receive your full card number or wallet credentials. The site stores payment references, order and tax amounts, and a hashed copy of your private order key to deliver downloads. When you use PayPal or Venmo, the site sends your billing address to Stripe Tax to calculate applicable sales tax. The site does not retain the address in its order database.

Keep your private order link with your purchase records. Anyone with the link can use it to download the edition you bought.

The Mac app

Net DNS Monitor writes incident reports and logs on your Mac. Optional alerting and diagnostic integrations can send information to the services you configure. The app’s privacy policy explains the data each edition handles.
