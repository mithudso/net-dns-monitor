Privacy

Website and purchases

Cloudflare serves this site and processes requests, including information such as your IP address. If you buy Premium, Stripe Managed Payments processes your payment and billing address under its own privacy policy. Net DNS Monitor does not receive your full card number or billing address. The site stores Stripe transaction references, your selected use, the order amount and status, and a hashed copy of your private order key to deliver downloads.

Keep your private order link with your purchase records. Anyone with the link can use it to download the edition you bought.

Google AdSense

The homepage loads Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, and other identifiers. Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

The Mac app

Net DNS Monitor writes incident reports and logs on your Mac. Optional alerting and diagnostic integrations can send information to the services you configure. The app’s privacy policy explains the data each edition handles.
